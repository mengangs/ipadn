compile_reveal_animations.py converts the original paths, transforms and easing
to native CAShapeLayer keyframes, normalizes the glyph size uniformly, and drops
only the entirely static tail. The Share artwork and lift/return/settle sequence
are original iPadn work, not Telegram artwork. Both use Telegram-inspired
play-once activation at visible content >= 0.1 and reset at <= 0.01.
